Your questions

What to know before you commit.

How does RPO differ from using a recruitment agency?

An agency engagement often centres on filling individual vacancies. With RPO, TASC manages an agreed part of your recruitment operation, including the delivery team, process and performance reporting. The scope can cover a project or ongoing hiring.

Will we lose control of hiring?

Your team keeps control of hiring priorities, budgets and final decisions. TASC manages the agreed recruitment activities, with clear approval points and regular reviews.

Do we have to replace our recruitment system?

We assess your existing system first. The delivery setup may use your applicant tracking system, TASC tools or a combination. Connections and access are confirmed during scoping.

Can we start with one project or business unit?

Yes. Project RPO can cover a defined hiring programme, while a wider engagement can focus on selected functions or locations. The appropriate scope depends on your hiring volume and timing.

How are fees structured?

Enterprise proposals can combine a monthly management fee with a fee per hire. Project pricing depends on the requirements. Your proposal sets out the team, technology, scope and charges before you commit.

How quickly can recruitment start?

We agree a start date after reviewing the roles, required team, system access and approval process. The implementation plan sets out what TASC and your team need to have ready.

How will candidate and company data be handled?

Data access, handling requirements and responsibilities form part of the agreed setup. Your IT and privacy teams can review these before implementation.

Which part of your hiring needs support?

Discuss your requirements with the RPO team and identify a suitable scope of support.
