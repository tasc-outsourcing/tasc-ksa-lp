How it works

How TASC runs your recruitment function.

We agree what your business needs from recruitment, assign the delivery team and establish how work will be managed and measured. The service evolves as hiring priorities and workforce demand change.

Capacity and accountability

Additional recruitment capacity or responsibility for the function?

An onsite recruiter adds capacity to your team. Enterprise RPO adds responsibility for an agreed recruitment process, its delivery and its performance.

Service commitments

Clear commitments on hiring speed, cost and quality.

Each engagement defines the measures, targets and review process used to assess recruitment delivery.

Time to fill

Time from approved vacancy to accepted offer.

What we agree

Targets by role group and how approval delays or paused vacancies are treated.

Cost per hire

Agreed recruitment costs divided by hires within the defined reporting scope.

What we agree

Included costs, the definition of a hire and the target or budget used for comparison.

Quality of hire

Agreed indicators such as hiring manager feedback and retention at six and twelve months.

What we agree

Assessment timing, feedback method and the employee groups being reviewed.
