    What is Our Approach to Permanent
    Recruitment Services?

    With a focus on improving the performance of your recruitment projects, TASC's priority is to deliver exceptional permanent recruitment services in Saudi Arabia. Our services are geared towards simplifying recruitment, enhancing efficiency, and developing a skilled workforce for your organization.

    As one of the established permanent recruitment firms, we use a four-step approach that covers all vital aspects of permanent recruitment to ensure your success –

    Customer Specifications and Needs

    When a client approaches us, our permanent recruitment consultant will take on the request and conduct several meetings to gain a comprehensive understanding of the project.

    In some instances, we also recommend a site visit to better understand the workplace environment and other nuances related to the request. We move on to the next stage of our service only after we have a clear understanding of your requirements, goals, company values, and workplace culture.

    Crafting a Staffing Plan

    After gaining a clear understanding of your requirements and suggestions, our officers move to the next step of developing a customized recruitment plan. The plan may involve advert optimization and onboarding procedures tailored to your specific needs.

    Our experts will explain every stage of the plan to you, allowing you to make changes wherever necessary. Once the plan is approved, we can begin the permanent recruitment process.

    Candidate Evaluation and Assessment

    Our recruitment experts will leverage their professional contacts, personal networks, and databases to locate the desired talent in this phase. Each candidate will undergo various screening processes, interviews, and background checks in accordance with the approved plan.

    Our officers will verify that every shortlisted candidate is a perfect fit for the company's culture and values throughout the process.

    Job Proposal and Orientation

    Following the shortlisting of the most deserving candidates, the officers will proceed to the post-selection activities, which include salary negotiations, candidate briefings, and offer letter issuance.

    Our permanent recruitment consultant in Saudi Arabia will also assist with onboarding to ensure that the hired candidates become productive as soon as possible.

    Our permanent recruitment services in Saudi Arabia are designed to provide clients with a complete and comprehensive recruitment experience. With our expert team, we cover everything from initial contact to candidate onboarding, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free process.
