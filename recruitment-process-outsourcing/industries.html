BUYING QUESTIONS

FAQs

Can TASC support recruitment across multiple Saudi cities?+

TASC has experience coordinating recruitment across Saudi locations. Delivery arrangements depend on hiring volumes, role requirements and the agreed recruitment scope.

How does RPO support specialist recruitment?+

An RPO programme can bring dedicated sourcing, candidate assessment, interview coordination and performance reporting around specialist hiring requirements.

How is recruitment performance measured?+

RPO performance can be reviewed through agreed measures such as time to fill, cost per hire, candidate movement and quality indicators.

Can TASC support Saudization requirements?+

TASC can support Saudi national recruitment through local talent networks, market knowledge and recruitment planning aligned with the agreed hiring requirements.

Can the RPO model change when hiring demand increases?+

Recruitment delivery capacity and responsibilities can be reviewed as hiring requirements change, subject to the agreed service model.
