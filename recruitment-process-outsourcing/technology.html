How the TASC ecosystem works

One operating model

  • Your business

    Sets workforce requirements, budgets and selection criteria, approves hires and makes final candidate decisions.

  • TASC recruitment team

    A named RPO lead and recruiters turn the brief into recruitment activity, apply judgment to candidates and own delivery against agreed targets.

  • Human and AI partnership

    AI support organises information and highlights where attention is needed. It does not replace accountability.

  • Your systems or ours

    Work alongside your existing applicant tracking system, subject to agreed technical requirements, or use TASC’s tools and delivery processes.

Connected tools

  • Hyrra

    AI-supported talent search, candidate flow and recruiter workflow across sourcing and assessment.

  • Recruitify

    Recruitment events, candidate registration and attendance.

  • Onboardify

    Joining requests, outstanding activities and status, with candidate details masked.

  • TASC Control Tower

    Built on TASC One. Time to fill, cost per hire, quality of hire and Saudization against agreed SLAs, with the actions that need follow through.

01Plan

Shape hiring around the business.

Your team sets workforce requirements, roles and priorities. TASC turns the agreed brief into search criteria and recruitment activity. Talent insight supports the conversation. Planning decisions remain with people.

02Source

Find relevant talent. Build your pipeline.

Recruiters use role and skill criteria to focus candidate searches, then review relevance and engage potential candidates.

03Assess

Keep candidate review and decisions moving.

Recruiters screen against your criteria and coordinate candidate review, interviews and offers. Hiring managers retain selection decisions.

04Onboard

Know what is ready. Follow up on what is not.

The recruitment team tracks joining requests and coordinates documentation and outstanding activities within the agreed scope. Workplace induction remains with your business.

05Measure and improve

Turn performance into the next decision.

Your RPO lead reviews hiring speed, cost and quality with your team, identifies issues and follows agreed actions. Targets are agreed for each engagement.
