RPO Control Tower

A management view configured for each client and built on TASC One. The indicators, targets and review rhythm are agreed during scoping. The dashboard on this page uses sample data to show how a review works.

See the sample dashboard

A shared view of hiring performance

The Control Tower gives your HR leaders and hiring managers a shared view of performance and the actions that need attention.

Use each review to identify actions, owners and the next point of follow through.

What we measure

Targets for each measure are agreed for every engagement and reviewed with your team.

01

Time to fill

Approved vacancy to accepted offer, averaged across offers accepted in the period.

02

Cost per hire

Recruitment costs within the agreed reporting scope, divided by hires joining in the period.

03

Quality of hire

Manager feedback and six month retention, reviewed as separate indicators.

04

Saudization

Saudi nationals as a share of hires, tracked against your Saudization targets.

Indicators clients can choose

Each Control Tower is configured around the measures your leadership reviews.

  • Open roles
  • Time to submit
  • Time to hire
  • Interview to offer
  • Offer acceptance
  • Source quality
  • Saudi share of hires
  • Roles ageing over 60 days

Questions it answers

  • Where are open roles stuck?
  • Are we meeting time to fill targets?
  • Where are offers being lost?
  • Are Saudi pipelines keeping pace with targets?
