Saudi capability building

Build capability. Build the future.

Localisation is not the same as capability. The objective is to solve today’s technical gap while deliberately transferring the work, decisions and operating ownership needed tomorrow.

01

International specialist

Deep experience is deployed against a defined capability gap.

02

Saudi counterpart

A named Saudi employee works beside the specialist on real tasks.

03

Practise and lead

Responsibility moves from observation to shared delivery and Saudi-led work.

04

Prove capability

Independent performance is assessed against clear operating standards.

05

Saudi ownership

Decisions and operational responsibility move with the capability.

Observe. Practise. Lead. Prove. Own.

Shadowing alone is not a transfer plan. Every priority capability needs an international owner, a Saudi successor, required live exposure, evidence of competence and a target handover point.

For overseas development, define the return role before departure and require the employee to apply the capability in Saudi Arabia after returning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does workforce mobilisation mean?

It is the coordinated process that takes a selected person through the right employment route, role and document checks, immigration, Saudi setup, arrival and project onboarding until they can legally and practically start work.

How many active mining licences are reported in Saudi Arabia?

The supplied 2026 workforce playbook reports 3,017 active mining licences at the end of March 2026, including 1,075 exploration licences.

Should Saudi and international workforce plans be separate?

No. The supplied playbooks recommend a blended plan that identifies which capability is available locally, which international depth is needed now and how Saudi capability can be developed beside it.
